Interesting Facts About Earth

Earth is an amazing planet with many interesting features. Here are some fascinating facts about our home planet.

Feature Information
🌍 Position from the Sun Third Planet
💧 Water Coverage About 71% of Earth's surface
🌙 Natural Satellite The Moon
🎂 Age About 4.5 Billion Years
🌎 Shape Nearly spherical
☀️ Distance from Sun About 150 Million km
⏰ Length of a Day About 24 Hours
📅 Length of a Year About 365.25 Days

Did You Know?

  • Earth is the only known planet with life.
  • Earth has one natural satellite, the Moon.
  • The atmosphere protects life from harmful radiation.
  • Earth's magnetic field helps protect the planet from solar wind.
  • Earth is constantly rotating on its axis.
