Earth is our home and the only known planet that supports life. It is a beautiful planet filled with oceans, forests, mountains, animals, and billions of people.


About Earth

Earth is the third planet from the Sun. It is the fifth-largest planet in our solar system and the only known planet to support life.

Why is Earth Special?

  • It has liquid water.
  • It has an atmosphere containing oxygen.
  • It supports millions of different species.
  • It has a suitable temperature for life.
